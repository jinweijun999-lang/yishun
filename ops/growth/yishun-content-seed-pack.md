# YiShun Content Seed Pack

## Positioning

YiShun is an Eastern timing and BaZi decision companion for self-reflection. It helps users turn a birth-chart based timing signal into one practical action, one avoid item, a saved report, and a shareable card.

All public copy must avoid deterministic promises. YiShun content is not financial advice, not medical advice, not legal advice, and not a guarantee of relationship, career, or investment outcomes.

## SEO Page Briefs

### Free BaZi Calculator

- Intent: users searching for free BaZi calculator, Chinese astrology chart, Four Pillars reading.
- Above-fold CTA: "Get today's timing signal."
- Internal links: samples, daily timing, membership, share landing examples.
- Conversion event: `reading_start_clicked`.

### Daily Chinese Horoscope

- Intent: users searching for daily horoscope with more practical timing.
- Above-fold CTA: "Generate a personal daily timing card."
- Internal links: reading start, reports library, share card.
- Conversion event: `daily_card_viewed`.

### Compatibility / Relationship

- Conversion event: `share_clicked`.

## Paid Flow Copy Guardrails

- Mention checkout only as the path to unlock a Full Report or membership.
- Never imply entitlement before webhook fulfillment.
- Ask users to save their report before checkout if payment is unavailable.
- If checkout fails, route users to support and report recovery, not a manual grant.
